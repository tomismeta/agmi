# Answer core guidance

Produce an original plain-text MinePOA answer that satisfies the supplied challenge constraints. Integrate every required word meaningfully, not as decoration.

Explain one plausible Sybil-resistance mechanism: identify the non-shareable per-identity cost, how the cost scales across identities, who verifies the mechanism, an explicit consequence of failed verification, and honest limitations. Binding numeric rules describe the proposed mechanism, not invented protocol facts or observed prices.

Avoid copying supplied history or examples. Answer history contains local previously submitted text, including unresolved submissions; it is not evidence of acceptance, publication, or rewards. Use the user guidance for voice, perspective, and originality without treating its claims as verified protocol facts.
